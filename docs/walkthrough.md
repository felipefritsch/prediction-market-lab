# Five-minute recruiting walkthrough

**Opening (30 seconds).** State the research question. Explain that this is an independent rebuild of an earlier monitoring idea. Identify the current demonstration as synthetic, or explicitly name the observed capture window and coverage if using qualified real data.

**Observation (one minute).** Open Universe and coverage. Show one reviewed regulation-time 1X2 family and all six assets. Explain receipt versus source time, versioned fees/rules and what the quality percentage actually measures. Point to a saved exclusion and a disconnect.

**Market behavior (one minute).** Open Fixture replay, move through a liquidity change, and compare quotes, depth and received scoreboard/evidence messages. Explain why a score receipt is not exact goal time and why public depth does not reveal queue position.

**Research (one minute).** Show fixed-horizon labels, the held-out fixture split and a simple baseline comparison. Explain event-level uncertainty and sparse-window exclusions. State a negative finding or insufficient sample plainly; do not infer edge from synthetic fitted results.

**Execution (one minute).** Compare latency and queue scenarios, inspect a partially filled/rejected order, and reconcile cash, inventory, fees and settlement. Explain why a quoted opportunity can disappear under depth/latency and why passive results remain modeled.

**Close (30 seconds).** Open a dossier: numerical change, then-known sources, missing evidence, and separately labeled later behavior. State the next empirical question, actual collection status and the uncertainty that remains. The strength of the project is the inspectable chain of evidence, not an unsupported profitability claim.
